How to study effectively:
  • Maths error notebook: for every mistake, write the question/topic, what went wrong (calculation, sign, concept or careless reading), the corrected steps, and redo it 2–3 days later.
  • Active recall: close the book and write or say what you remember. Revisit difficult material after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days.
  • Hindi movies: use Hindi audio and subtitles; repeat three lines aloud, then retell the scene in your own Hindi.
  • Weekly test: once each weekend, take a short timed test on topics already taught, then spend time correcting every error.
  • Grade 8 IIT foundation: master school algebra, fractions, equations, geometry, logical reasoning and Science concepts before rushing into senior-grade JEE material. A schedule cannot guarantee admission.
